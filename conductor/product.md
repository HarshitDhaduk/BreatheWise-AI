# Product Definition - BreatheWise AI

## Project Name
**BreatheWise AI** (Urban Air Quality & Winter Smog Copilot for Families and Communities)

## Project Description
A GenAI-powered assistant that empowers families and communities to navigate severe winter smog and urban air pollution through low-emission daily routines, indoor air quality hacks, and sustainable alternatives tailored to their location and lifestyle, while clearly explaining the direct impact of their choices.

## Problem Statement
During severe winter months, cities experience severe temperature inversions that trap particulates ($PM_{2.5}$, $PM_{10}$, $NO_2$, soot) at ground level, pushing the Air Quality Index (AQI) into hazardous territory (300 to 500+). Families lack actionable, personalized daily plans, cannot afford high-end air filtration systems, and lack clear understanding of how their daily commute, heating, and cooking choices impact their lung health and city emissions.

## Target Personas
1. **Urban Parents & Caregivers**: Protecting infants and school children from severe smog.
2. **High-Risk Individuals**: Asthmatics, COPD patients, elderly family members.
3. **Daily Urban Commuters**: Navigating high-exposure routes and adopting low-emission options.
4. **Community & Apartment Groups**: Coordinating collective smog mitigation, waste burning prevention, and carpooling.

## Key Goals
1. Provide real-time hyper-local air intelligence with thermal inversion detection and clean-air ventilation window prediction.
2. Generate context-aware, low-emission daily routines (school timings, commute modal shifts, exercise safety).
3. Offer an interactive DIY Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) lab (e.g., Corsi-Rosenthal boxes, weatherstripping, cooking protocols).
4. Deliver an Impact & Explainability Engine that calculates both personal health burden avoided ($PM_{2.5}$ micrograms, cigarette equivalents) and collective emissions saved ($CO_2$, $NO_x$).
5. Power an interactive, grounded GenAI Copilot using Google Gemini models with offline domain-knowledge resilience.
