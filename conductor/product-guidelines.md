# Product Guidelines - BreatheWise AI

## Voice and Tone
- **Empathetic & Calming**: Acknowledge the anxiety severe smog causes for families without inducing panic.
- **Scientifically Grounded**: Back recommendations with WHO, EPA, and pulmonary health facts.
- **Empowering & Action-Oriented**: Focus on what users *can* do (low-cost hacks, timing shifts, community pledges) rather than fatalistic warnings.
- **Transparent & Quantifiable**: Always connect actions to concrete impact numbers (e.g., "$84\,\mu g$ of $PM_{2.5}$ saved", "equivalent to 2.4 cigarettes unsmoked").

## Design Principles
1. **Clarity Over Clutter**: Color-coded, instantly readable AQI states using accessible, colorblind-friendly palettes.
2. **Inclusive & Low Barrier**: Highlight low-cost and DIY alternatives (costing under \$30) alongside standard equipment.
3. **Privacy First & Local Storage**: Family profiles and health vulnerabilities are stored locally on the client's device; no unnecessary telemetry.
4. **Resilient Architecture**: Zero-downtime offline fallback so core features and advice work seamlessly even during network degradation.
